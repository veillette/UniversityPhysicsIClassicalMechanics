# University Physics I: Classical Mechanics

Julio Gea-Banacloche  
University of Arkansas, Fayetteville

This is a web-native MyST Markdown edition of the Fall 2019 revision of
*University Physics I: Classical Mechanics*. The book introduces classical
mechanics for scientists and engineers, followed by waves and a brief
introduction to thermodynamics.

This edition preserves the content of the source textbook. Its canonical
source is the MyST Markdown in this repository.
